All is solved in 2D real spaces.

This equation has much much better convergence using scipy.optimize.root

0 = f := (k1/eps1 - k2/eps2) * (k1/eps1 - k3/eps3) * cmath.exp(-2*k1*t) - (k1/eps1 + k2/eps2) * (k1/eps1 + k3/eps3)      

where

d := (k1/eps1 - k2/eps2) * (k1/eps1 - k3/eps3)

is the original denominator. We then have to analyze roots where d is "near" 0 seperately (dirty definition).
