# Purpose of this project

You may find a collection of programs aiming to develop control over the formation of Laser-Induced Periodic Surface Structures (LIPSS) based on the assumption that LIPSS are formed by excitation of Surface Plasmon Polaritons (SPPs). Demonstation of such possibility are given into the following references: 

* http://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00339-013-8205-2#page-1

* http://scitation.aip.org/content/aip/journal/jap/114/8/10.1063/1.4818433

* http://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00339-013-8205-2

* http://scitation.aip.org/content/aip/journal/jap/116/7/10.1063/1.4887808

To reach this purpose, it is planned to collect and develop database for many materials on: 

* laser induced damage thresholds (LIDT), 

* SPP theory for single and multilayer systems,
